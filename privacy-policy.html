1. Introduction

Diamond Edge Metals Inc. ("Diamond Edge Metals", "we", "our", or "us") provides industrial logistics, scrap material grading, workforce dispatch, and real-time fleet telemetrics via the Diamond Edge Metals mobile application and web portal. We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding how your data is collected, processed, and secured.

2. Data We Collect

Depending on your role (Scrap Seller, Certified Driver, Fleet Boss, or Yard Supervisor), we collect the following categories of information:

  • Account & Identity Data: Full legal name, email address, phone number, vehicle specifications, commercial driver license (CDL) numbers, and enterprise EID identification codes.
  • Precise & Coarse Geolocation Data: With user permission (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION), we stream real-time driver coordinates during active logistics assignments to provide depot ETA calculations and secure live client dispatch tracking links.
  • Camera & Visual Data: Images captured through the camera (CAMERA) for spectroscopic AI material alloy classification, catalytic converter serial barcode identification, and digital scale ticket verification. Photographs are transmitted securely to our certified valuation backend (https://realdiamondmetals-valuation-api.onrender.com) for real-time metallurgical valuation.
  • Microphone & Audio Diagnostics Data: Audio input (RECORD_AUDIO) is captured ephemerally in volatile memory (RAM) for real-time speech diagnostics and voice synthesizer testing only. Audio streams are never stored on persistent storage (disk or database) and are never transmitted to third parties or remote servers.
  • Device Identifiers & Advertising ID (AD_ID): In accordance with Google Play Store policies and the com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID permission, our app integrates Google AdMob (App ID: ca-app-pub-4285355284769713~8920863465). AdMob collects the Google Advertising ID for fraud prevention, analytics, frequency capping, and ad delivery. Users can opt out of personalized ads at any time via Android Settings > Google > Ads or via Google Ads Settings.
  • Transactional & Scale Metrics: Weight tickets, material grades, live spot market valuations, and digital bill-of-lading records.
  • Optional AI Training Scan Contribution (Default Off): Scrap metal photographs, canonical 30-class ISRI grade confirmations, surface condition tags (clean, dirty, or pgm_assay), and optional scale-ticket weights are collected only if you explicitly enable Version 1.0 Opt-In Training Consent. Before transmission, client-side canvas re-encoding strips embedded EXIF GPS coordinates, timestamps, and camera serial metadata.

3. How We Use Your Data

Collected information is used strictly to power core logistics and yard operations:

  • Dispatching flatbed pickups and updating Samsara IoT telemetric simulation feeds.
  • Computing EPA WARM lifecycle greenhouse gas emissions abatements and ESG audit certificates.
  • Validating enterprise driver seat allocations and hot-swappable license quotas.
  • Processing in-app subscription entitlements via RevenueCat and Google Play Billing.
  • Preventing unauthorized access and enforcing yard safety protocols.
  • Quarantined Dataset Curation & Promotional Scan Credits: Explicitly opted-in scrap scans pass automated domain and duplicate screening into an isolated quarantine_pending review queue prior to 30-class model training (unlocking 7+ promotional Pro AI scans per 5 verified scale-matched contributions without altering Google Play Billing subscriptions). You may revoke consent or permanently erase contributed scans in one tap from Settings or via DELETE /api/vision/opt-in-scan/:contributionId.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We disclose data solely to authenticated enterprise providers essential for platform operations:

  • Google Cloud & Firebase: For encrypted database persistence (Cloud Firestore), authentication, and hosting.
  • RevenueCat & Google Play: For subscription license validation and entitlement management.
  • Google Maps Platform: For route calculations and fleet marker geolocation rendering.
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): To deliver advertisements within the app, AdMob (App ID: ca-app-pub-4285355284769713~8920863465) processes device identifiers (including Advertising ID AD_ID) and diagnostic metrics. Users can manage ad preferences in Android Settings > Google > Ads.
  • Render Valuation API (Render Services, Inc.): Scrap material photographs and spectroscopic grade requests are securely processed at https://realdiamondmetals-valuation-api.onrender.com to compute density, alloy grade, and market valuations. No account credentials or financial data are transferred.

Google User Data

When you sign in with Google, Diamond Metals requests your basic profile information: name, email address, and profile photo (OAuth scopes openid, email, profile). This data is used solely to authenticate you and to link your dispatches, scans, and messages to your account.

If you choose to connect Google Drive from the Drive Portal or ESG Sustainability Ledger, the app separately requests Google Drive and Google Sheets access. This access is used only to save scrap tickets, scan photos, quotes, LME rate sheets, and sustainability ledgers that you create into your own Drive and Sheets; to list, organize, and delete those files from within the app at your request; and to display your Drive storage quota. Drive and Sheets content is not copied to our servers, and it is never used for advertising or to train AI models.

We do not sell Google user data, do not use it for advertising, and do not transfer it to third parties except as required to operate the service (Google Firebase Authentication and Firestore) or by law. Diamond Metals' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or delete your data through the Account Deletion Request Portal.

5. Data Retention & Security

All data in transit is encrypted using industry-standard TLS 1.3/SSL 256-bit encryption. Data at rest is protected by strict Firestore Security Rules. We retain personal and fleet information only for as long as your account remains active or as required by commercial tax and salvage yard record-keeping laws.

6. Your Rights & Account Deletion

You have the right to inspect, update, or permanently delete your personal information and driver records. In compliance with Google Play Developer policies, you can request full account and data erasure at any time without needing to reinstall the app by visiting our public Account Deletion Request Portal.
